Upload Rules

Effective July 28, 2026 · Last updated July 28, 2026

By uploading, you confirm that you have the right to store the content and that it complies with these rules.

1. Your right to store content

Upload only material you created, own, are licensed or authorized to use, or may otherwise lawfully store. An image being online does not make it free to use. Keep source attribution when appropriate.

2. Rights and privacy

Do not upload copyright infringement, unauthorized commercial imagery, trademark misuse, impersonation, fraud, nonconsensual intimate imagery, identity documents belonging to others, or material that violates another person’s privacy, publicity, or image rights.

3. Safety

Do not upload illegal content, malware, spam, threats, harassment, hateful abuse, sexual exploitation of minors, or files disguised as supported images.

4. Technical limits

Do not probe, overwhelm, bypass, or exploit upload infrastructure. Lacqr enforces supported image MIME types and a 10 MB object limit. The app currently converts selected images to JPEG before upload. Optional source links must use HTTP or HTTPS and are length-limited; Lacqr’s backend does not retrieve their contents.

5. Enforcement and review

Lacqr may investigate, remove or disable content, and restrict accounts where reasonably necessary. Serious or repeated violations may result in suspension or termination. Email lacqr.app@gmail.com to request review.
