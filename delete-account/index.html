Account controls

Delete your Lacqr account

Last updated July 27, 2026

You can permanently delete your account from inside Lacqr. This action cannot be undone.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Lacqr and sign in.
  2. Select Profile.
  3. Open Settings in the top-right corner.
  4. Under Account, select Delete Account.
  5. Review the deletion explanation, type DELETE, and confirm.

The app reports success only after the authenticated deletion process confirms completion.

What is removed

  • Your Lacqr authentication account and session.
  • User-owned Closet, Inspiration, Boards, Wishlist, Appointment, profile, note, tag, rating, and history records supported by the service.
  • Private Storage files owned by your account.
  • Account-scoped local data, drafts, image cache, session data, and scheduled Lacqr reminders on the device completing deletion.

Limited retention

Deletion is designed to remove active account data. Limited information may be retained where required by law, necessary to investigate a security issue or unresolved transaction, or present temporarily in encrypted backups until the applicable backup cycle expires.

If you cannot access the app

Email lacqr.app@gmail.com from the address connected to your account. Lacqr may need to verify control of the account before acting on a request. Never send your password.

Before deleting
Use Profile → Settings → Export My Data if you want a copy of the currently supported structured records. The current export does not include private image bytes.
