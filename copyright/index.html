Copyright Policy

Effective July 28, 2026 · Last updated July 28, 2026

Lacqr respects creators’ rights. Private visibility does not remove a user’s responsibility to store material lawfully.

1. User responsibility

Users retain ownership of their content and may store only material they created, own, are licensed or authorized to use, or may otherwise lawfully store. An image being available online does not make it free to use. Lacqr does not endorse infringement.

2. Reporting a copyright concern

Email lacqr.app@gmail.com. A complete report should include:

  • A physical or electronic signature of an authorized person.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work, or a representative list.
  • Identification and enough location information for Lacqr to find the material.
  • Your name, address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized by the owner, agent, or law.
  • A statement that the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act.

Do not send passwords, tokens, or unrelated private user information.

3. Review, removal, and repeat infringement

Lacqr may validate the report, preserve limited evidence, remove or disable access to identified material, notify the affected user, and restrict or terminate accounts for serious or repeated infringement. Lacqr may request missing information or legal review before acting.

4. Counter-notices

If material was removed by mistake or misidentification, email the same address with:

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the removed material and its former location.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that removal resulted from mistake or misidentification.
  • Your name, address, and telephone number.
  • Consent to the court jurisdiction and service-of-process requirements applicable under relevant law.

Lacqr may forward a counter-notice as required and may wait the legally required period or seek legal review before restoration.

5. Status of this policy

This is a general Copyright Policy. Lacqr does not currently claim that a DMCA designated agent is registered or that safe-harbor requirements have been completed. Such a claim should be made only after registration, publication of approved agent details, and operation of the notice and repeat-infringer process.
