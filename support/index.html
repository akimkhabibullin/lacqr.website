Support

For the Lacqr iPhone app

Lacqr has no account and stores everything on your iPhone, so most answers below are about your device.

Contact

Email lacqr.app@gmail.com. Tell us which screen you were on, what happened, and what you expected. A screenshot helps.

Back up and restore

In Lacqr, open Profile → Settings → Back Up & Restore and tap Back up now. Save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. To restore, choose Restore from a backup and pick that file. Restoring replaces what’s currently in Lacqr.

Moving to a new iPhone

If you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, Lacqr’s data comes with it. Otherwise, make a Lacqr backup on the old phone, then restore it on the new one.

Photos and camera

Lacqr uses the iOS photo picker, so it only sees the photos you choose. If the camera doesn’t open, check iOS Settings → Lacqr → Camera.

Reminders

Turn on reminders in Profile → Settings → Notifications. If they don’t appear, check iOS Settings → Notifications → Lacqr and any Focus mode you use.

Sharing into Lacqr

In Safari or another app, tap Share and choose Lacqr. The link opens in Lacqr ready to save to a board. If Lacqr isn’t listed, tap More in the share sheet and turn it on.

Erase your data

Open Profile → Settings → Erase All Data, or delete the app. Lacqr has no copy of your data, so this can’t be undone unless you’ve made a backup.
