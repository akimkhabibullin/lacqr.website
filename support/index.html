Help

Support

For the Lacqr iPhone app

For account-specific help, contact us from the email address connected to your Lacqr account. Never send your password, access token, or private image link.

Contact Lacqr

Email lacqr.app@gmail.com. Include the app screen, what happened, what you expected, and the steps you already tried. You may attach a screenshot after checking that it contains no information you do not want to share.

Response times are not guaranteed. Account-access and security reports are prioritized.

Basic troubleshooting

  1. Confirm your iPhone has a working internet connection.
  2. Pull down to refresh Closet or Inspiration.
  3. Close and reopen Lacqr.
  4. Confirm that you are signed into the intended account.
  5. Install the latest available version of Lacqr and iOS.

Sign-in or password problems

Use the Password option in Profile → Settings to request a password-reset email. Check the address carefully and review spam or junk folders. If the message does not arrive, contact support without including your password.

Camera or photo access

Open iOS Settings → Lacqr to review camera and photo permissions. Lacqr requests access only after you choose an image action. The rest of your collection remains available if you decline photo access.

Export your data

Open Profile → Settings → Export My Data. The current export uses the iOS share sheet to provide structured account and collection records. Private image bytes are not included in the current export; private storage references are included without exposing temporary signed URLs.

Delete your account

Account deletion is available directly inside Lacqr. See the complete account-deletion instructions. Deletion is permanent and should be used only after saving anything you want to keep.

Report a security or privacy issue

Email lacqr.app@gmail.com with a clear description. Do not include passwords, session tokens, service credentials, or private signed image URLs.
