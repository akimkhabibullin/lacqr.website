Privacy controls

Privacy Choices

Effective July 28, 2026 · Last updated July 28, 2026

Use these controls to review, correct, export, or delete information associated with your Lacqr account.

1. Access and correction

Review saved records in Closet, Inspo, and Profile. Update your display name from Profile. For another account-data question, email lacqr.app@gmail.com from the account address. Lacqr may need to verify account control.

2. Export supported data

Open Profile → Settings → Export My Data. The app assembles structured JSON text on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. No server-side export file is created. Current exports include supported records and stable private-storage references, but not private image bytes or expiring signed URLs.

3. Delete content or the account

Delete individual items from their edit screens. For full deletion, open Profile → Settings → Delete Account, type DELETE, and confirm. Deletion is irreversible. See the full deletion instructions.

4. Permissions and reminders

Manage camera, photo-library, and notification access in iOS Settings. In Lacqr Settings, choose whether to schedule local appointment or weekly reminders. Lacqr currently does not register a remote push token or send marketing notifications.

5. Privacy requests and review

Describe your request without sending a password, token, or private signed link. Reasonable identity verification may be required. Lacqr will respond as required by applicable law. If you disagree with a response, reply and ask for review.

6. Backups and support correspondence

Deletion removes active account data. Copies may remain temporarily in configured provider or encrypted backup cycles before aging out. Support email is separate from the app account and is not automatically erased by in-app deletion; you may ask for that correspondence to be reviewed.
