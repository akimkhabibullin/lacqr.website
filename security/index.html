Security

Protecting private collections

Last updated July 28, 2026

Lacqr uses layered controls for account records and private photos while recognizing that no product can promise perfect security.

Protection approach

  • Authenticated accounts and user-owned database records.
  • Row-level separation for supported tables.
  • Private, account-scoped photo storage and short-lived display access.
  • Validated image formats and server-side file-size limits.
  • Encrypted network transport.
  • Account-scoped session, cache, draft, temporary-file, and reminder cleanup on logout and deletion.

Deletion removes active account records and private files through an authenticated server-side process.

Report a security concern

Email lacqr.app@gmail.com. Include the affected feature, reproducible steps, impact, and app version if known. Do not include credentials, tokens, private signed links, or another user’s content.

Responsible security research

Do not access, modify, retain, or disclose another person’s information; disrupt the service; use automated high-volume testing; or attempt extortion. Stop testing and report promptly if private information becomes visible.

Response and limits

Credible security and account-access reports are prioritized, but no specific response or remediation time is promised. Backups and recovery reduce risk but cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability or recovery of every item.
