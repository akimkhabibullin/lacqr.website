Privacy Policy

Last updated July 27, 2026

Lacqr is a private nail-collection and inspiration organizer. This policy explains what information Lacqr processes, why it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

1. Information you provide

We process the email address used for your account, your Supabase account identifier, and an optional display name. We also process the closet entries, inspiration, boards, wishlist items, appointments, ratings, notes, tags, wear history, source links, and photos that you choose to save.

2. How information is collected

Account and collection information is received when you create an account or save content. Camera and photo-library content is accessed only after you choose the corresponding action and grant the requested iOS permission.

Infrastructure providers automatically process limited connection and security information, such as IP address, user agent, request time and status, and authentication events, to deliver and protect the service. Lacqr does not request contacts, precise location, microphone access, or tracking permission.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, keep each user’s collection separate, sync and display saved content, provide reminders you enable, export supported data, respond to support requests, protect the service, and complete deletion requests.

4. Service providers and sharing

Supabase provides authentication, database, private file-storage, and security logging infrastructure. Apple and Expo may process technical information required to distribute and run the application. Vercel processes limited connection information to host, deliver, and protect the public Lacqr website. These service providers are expected to protect data consistently with their contractual and legal obligations.

Lacqr does not sell personal information, show third-party advertising, use cross-app tracking, or share uploaded content with data brokers.

5. Private photos and links

Uploaded images are stored in a private bucket under your account identifier. The app uses short-lived signed access URLs for display; those temporary URLs are not the permanent source of truth. Optional web links are stored for attribution and convenience. Lacqr does not ask its backend to scrape or download content from those links.

6. Retention and deletion

Account information and user content are retained while your account remains active. You can delete individual content in the app. Delete Account permanently removes the authentication account, user-owned database records, and private Storage objects. Local account data, cached images, sessions, drafts, and scheduled Lacqr reminders are cleared as part of deletion.

Limited records may be retained only when required by law, security, or an unresolved transaction. Temporary backup copies may remain until the applicable backup cycle expires.

7. Your choices

You may update your display name, manage iOS permissions, change notification preferences, export supported account data, delete individual content, sign out, or permanently delete your account from Settings. For privacy access, correction, or deletion questions, email lacqr.app@gmail.com.

8. Security

Lacqr uses authenticated access, row-level security, private object storage, expiring signed URLs, encrypted transport, and account-scoped local cleanup. No system can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password and contact us if you believe your account has been compromised.

9. Children

Lacqr is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed.

10. Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected in this policy and its updated date before new data practices are introduced. Privacy questions may be sent to lacqr.app@gmail.com.
