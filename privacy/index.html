Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026 · Last updated September 30, 2026

Lacqr is an iPhone app for your nail polish, press-ons, and inspiration. It works without an account and keeps everything on your device.

1. Summary

Lacqr does not collect your personal information. There is no account and no sign-up. Everything you save stays on your iPhone, and Lacqr has no servers that receive it. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

2. What stays on your iPhone

Your polishes, press-ons, inspiration, boards, notes, ratings, wear history, photos, optional name, onboarding answers, and reminder settings are stored only in Lacqr on your device. The developer cannot see, access, or recover them.

3. No third-party services

Lacqr contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking tools, and shares nothing with third parties. The app does not need an internet connection to work.

4. Photos and camera

Lacqr uses the iOS photo picker, which gives Lacqr only the photos you choose. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo. Chosen photos are resized and saved inside Lacqr on your iPhone.

5. Sharing into Lacqr

When you share a web page into Lacqr from another app, Lacqr saves the link and title you shared. Lacqr does not visit the link or download anything from it.

6. Notifications

If you turn them on, Lacqr schedules reminders on your iPhone (a weekly reminder and polish expiration reminders). These are local notifications. No push token is created and nothing is sent to a server.

7. Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Apple includes Lacqr’s data in that backup under Apple’s terms. If you use Back Up & Restore, Lacqr creates a file that you choose where to save, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Lacqr never uploads it anywhere.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete any item in the app, erase everything with Settings → Erase All Data, or delete the app. Backup files you saved yourself are not affected.

9. Your rights

Because Lacqr holds no personal information about you, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You stay in full control: export your data with Back Up & Restore, and delete it in the app at any time. If you emailed support, you can ask for that correspondence to be deleted.

10. Contacting support

If you email lacqr.app@gmail.com, we receive whatever you choose to send, such as your email address and message, and use it only to reply. Email is sent from your own Mail app.

11. The website

The Lacqr website has no ads, analytics, forms, or cookies. Its host, Vercel, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security and operation.

12. Children

Lacqr is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

13. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the update will be posted in the app and on the website. Questions: lacqr.app@gmail.com.
