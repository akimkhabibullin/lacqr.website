Privacy Policy

Effective July 28, 2026 · Last updated July 28, 2026

This policy explains the information processed through the Lacqr iPhone app, public website, and support communications. Lacqr is a private nail-collection and inspiration organizer; user content is private by default and is not a public profile or searchable social feed.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to Lacqr’s app, website, and related support. Lacqr currently operates under the Lacqr name. Privacy questions and legal notices may be sent to lacqr.app@gmail.com.

2. Information processed

Account and profile

Email address, Supabase account identifier, and optional display name.

Collection and user content

Polish and press-on details, inspiration, boards, wishlist items, appointments, ratings, notes, tags, wear history, colors, source links, prices, salons, technicians, and photos you choose to save.

Support

Sender address, message, and any attachments you provide when contacting support, privacy, copyright, or security.

Service information

Limited authentication, request, connection, and security records, which can include IP address, user agent, event time, request status, and error information.

3. How information is collected

Information is received when you register, edit your profile, save or import content, schedule a reminder, request export or deletion, visit the website, or contact Lacqr. Camera and photo-library content is accessed only after you choose the corresponding action and grant iOS permission. Infrastructure providers automatically create limited delivery and security logs.

4. Purposes

  • Authenticate accounts and keep each user’s records separate.
  • Sync, store, and display saved collection content.
  • Schedule local reminders the user enables.
  • Create a requested on-device export.
  • Answer support, privacy, copyright, and security communications.
  • Prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, protect the service, and complete deletion.

5. Service providers and disclosures

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, private file storage, and related service logs.
  • Vercel hosts the public website and processes connection information needed to deliver and protect it.
  • Gmail processes correspondence sent to the Lacqr contact address.
  • Apple distributes the iPhone app and provides device permissions, local notification scheduling, and the iOS share sheet.

An independent S3-compatible backup provider processes encrypted object backups only if the production backup workflow is activated. Lacqr does not sell or rent personal information, use cross-app tracking, show third-party behavioral advertising, or provide uploaded content to data brokers.

6. Private photos, links, and AI

Uploaded images are stored in private, account-scoped storage. Short-lived signed URLs are created for display and are not kept as the permanent source of truth. Optional links are normalized and stored for attribution or convenience; Lacqr’s backend does not scrape or fetch them.

Lacqr does not use private uploaded photos to train generative AI models. The current production app does not contain an AI provider integration.

7. Permissions and notifications

Camera, photo-library, and notification permissions are optional. Manage them in iOS Settings. Lacqr currently schedules only local appointment and weekly reminders selected by the user. It does not register a remote push token or send marketing notifications. Turning off a permission prevents the related action but does not delete content already saved.

8. Website and support email

The public website currently uses no advertising or analytics script, account form, account cookie, or browser local storage. Vercel may retain website delivery and security logs. Support is handled through Gmail. Deleting a Lacqr account does not automatically erase a separate support-email thread; you may request review of that correspondence.

9. Data export

Profile → Settings → Export My Data assembles structured JSON text on the device and opens the iOS share sheet. Lacqr does not create or temporarily retain a server-side export file. Current exports include supported account and collection records and stable private-storage references, but not private image bytes or expiring signed URLs.

10. Retention, backups, and deletion

Active account records and user content remain until deleted. In-app account deletion first removes the user’s private Storage objects, then deletes the authenticated account; ownership relationships cascade deletion to user-owned database rows. The completing device clears the session, account-scoped local records, drafts, temporary files, image caches, and scheduled Lacqr reminders.

Provider-managed database backups or encrypted object backups may retain copies temporarily until their configured cycle expires. Limited complaint, support, security, or legal records may be retained where required or reasonably necessary, subject to applicable law.

11. International processing

Providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Infrastructure locations and legally required transfer mechanisms depend on provider account configuration and applicable law.

12. Your choices and requests

You may update your display name, manage iOS permissions and reminders, export supported data, delete individual items, or permanently delete the account. See Privacy Choices. Account-specific requests may require reasonable identity verification. Reply to a completed request to ask for review.

13. Security

Lacqr uses authenticated access, row-level record separation, private object storage, short-lived signed access, validated image uploads, encrypted network transport, and account-scoped device cleanup. No system guarantees absolute security. Use a unique password and follow the security reporting process if you suspect compromise.

14. Children and minors

Lacqr is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. The account flow does not collect a birth date. Users aged 13–17 may use Lacqr only with any parent or guardian consent required where they live.

15. Changes and contact

Material changes will be identified by a revised date and, where required, additional notice or consent rather than only a silent page edit. Contact lacqr.app@gmail.com.
